Map results and an AI answer55% the map results’ points plus 45% the answer’s; 60/40 when the map results sit above the answer, 45/55 when the answer sits above them.
Ads above the resultsAfter ads: the score × 0.88 for one, × 0.75 for two or more. A Local Services Ads block counts once, each sponsored result once.

Search visibility is the average score over a search’s points; the headline averages the latest scan of each search that doesn’t name the business. Weighted by household income, each point counts by its census tract’s income band (× 0.6 under $40k to × 1.85 at $150k and up) times its households (ACS 5-year).

Change compares each point with its average over the previous 14 days, on points searched at the same spot (within 150 m), so a larger grid never reads as a loss. Answers that couldn’t be read are left out; where earlier answers were read the older way, only the map results are compared.

Money. Verified actions come from the business’s own Google accounts, read-only. Prices are Google Ads averages for the county over the last 12 months (DataForSEO). Modeled figures use sample inputs until the business sets its own.
